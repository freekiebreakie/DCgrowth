Highest priority — do this first

Fix the funnel before spending on acquisition

We have 2M registered users and 6,323 active traders. Until we know where the 99.6% drop off, every euro spent on new users partially fills a leaky bucket. This is the first 30 days.

The rule: Do not scale any paid acquisition channel until registered → funded conversion is above 5%. Right now it is 0.4%. Fixing this is the highest-leverage work in the entire plan.

Measurement — instrument the funnel
Define and instrument every funnel step in analytics
Map the 5 key events: (1) Registration complete, (2) Onboarding complete, (3) First paper trade, (4) Deposit screen opened, (5) First real trade. Ensure every step fires a distinct analytics event so you can see exactly where drop-off happens. Do this in the first 3 days.
Week 1
Pull conversion report: registration → each subsequent step
From the analytics data, build a simple table: what % of registrations reach step 2? Step 3? The deposit screen? First real trade? Find the single biggest drop-off point. That is what gets fixed first. Everything else waits.
Week 1
Set up deposit abandonment tracking
Tag the event when a user opens the deposit screen and then navigates away without completing. This group deserves a separate reactivation flow — they are the highest-intent unconverted users we have.
Week 1Product
Onboarding — reduce time to first value
Measure time from install to first paper trade
This single number defines onboarding health. Target: under 3 minutes. If it is above 5, every screen you remove improves conversion. Identify the 2 lowest-value onboarding screens and test removing them.
Week 1Product
Move interest picker to the first onboarding screen
The interest picker (pre-IPO, stocks, commodities, crypto) should be the very first personalization step. When someone picks "pre-IPO companies," the next screen should immediately show SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic with live prices. The hook is the product — use it before asking for anything else.
Month 1Product
Build a guided first trade experience for new users
After the interest picker, suggest one specific asset ("You picked tech stocks — Tesla is up 2.4% today") and walk them through a paper trade step by step. Do not leave them to explore. Users who are hand-held through their first trade convert to funded at 3–5x the rate of self-navigators.
Month 1Product
A/B test: remove vs. keep the most friction-heavy onboarding screen
Run a 50/50 split. Measure completion rate of that step AND first paper trade rate downstream. A single screen removal can meaningfully shift conversion. Do not guess — test.
